Market Segments Guide

New Delhi is divided into 5 distinct food hubs based on coordinate distributions.

Cluster 0: Premium Centers

High-end hotspots (Avg Cost: ₹637). Dominated by premium restaurants with average ratings above 2.7.

Cluster 1: Outlier Zone

Isolated/unexplored locales. Ideal for carving out exclusive regional concepts.

Cluster 2: Commercial Core

Active business hubs (Avg Cost: ₹724, Rating: 2.67). Dense competition but massive foot traffic.

Cluster 3: Mid-Tier Suburbs

Residential suburbs (Avg Cost: ₹472). Focus on value-for-money, family packs, and deliveries.

Cluster 4: Local Budgets

Quick Service & stalls (Avg Cost: ₹416). Highly sensitive to pricing, rely on high volume.
